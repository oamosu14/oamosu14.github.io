Executive Overview

SOC-AI Forge was designed to bridge the gap between infrastructure engineering and security operations by bringing together the technologies and workflows commonly found in enterprise environments. Rather than deploying isolated tools, the platform demonstrates how monitoring, centralized logging, identity management, automation, threat intelligence, and incident response operate together as a cohesive system. It provides a practical environment for engineering, operational monitoring, investigation, and continuous improvement while emphasizing repeatable processes and operational consistency.

Platform Architecture

SOC-AI Forge follows a layered platform architecture that combines infrastructure services, security operations, observability, identity management, automation, and AI-assisted workflows into a unified operational environment. The platform is designed to simulate how modern enterprise security teams deploy, monitor, investigate, and maintain production systems.

Engineering Outcomes

SOC-AI Forge demonstrates the design, deployment, and operation of a modern enterprise-inspired Security Operations Center. The platform integrates infrastructure engineering, observability, centralized monitoring, security analytics, identity management, automation, and incident response into a cohesive operational environment. It serves as a practical engineering platform for developing real-world operational workflows while strengthening skills across Linux administration, platform engineering, DevSecOps, and cybersecurity operations.
